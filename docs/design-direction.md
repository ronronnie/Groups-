# Design Direction

## Visual Position

The interface should be clean, modern, and highly usable with restrained Pop Art personality.

Use the 70/20/10 principle:

- 70% calm neutral surfaces, excellent typography, white or off-white backgrounds, near-black text, and readable information architecture
- 20% brand colors, graphic borders, distinctive cards, navigation highlights, and branded illustrations
- 10% bold pop-art moments, halftones, stickers, expressive typography, purposeful animation, celebration states, AI moments, and reputation badges

Do not make every screen colorful. Avoid visual fatigue.

## Usability Standard

The product should be usable by younger job seekers and early or mid-career professionals, while still simple enough that a 60-year-old can join and understand key flows.

Use WhatsApp-level obviousness as the usability benchmark, but do not imitate WhatsApp as a product model.

## Navigation

Core Jobs group navigation:

- For You
- Jobs
- Tracker
- People
- Chat

Ask this Group should be available contextually across the experience, not isolated as a separate AI Chat page.

## AI Presentation

AI should appear in context as Brain. It should help users understand, compare, extract, search, and act.

Avoid a floating chatbot pattern. Brain is an intelligence layer embedded in product flows.
